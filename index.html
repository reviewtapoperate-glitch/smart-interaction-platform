FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the customer need an app?

No. The goal is to open the smart destination in the phone browser. NFC behaviour varies by device, so every card includes a QR fallback.

Can I change what my card opens?

On smart-link plans, yes. The physical card can continue pointing to the same ReviewTap URL while the destination changes.

Can I use my own Google review link?

Yes. You provide the official review link for your Business Profile.

Can you guarantee 5-star reviews?

No. ReviewTap should be used to make it easier for genuine customers to leave honest feedback. Businesses must not offer incentives or selectively solicit positive reviews.
